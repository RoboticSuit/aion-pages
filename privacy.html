Aion · the notice

Notice

This instrument belongs to one keeper and answers to no other.

What it reaches

Only what its single keeper already holds under their own name, entered and read on their behalf alone.

What becomes of it

Nothing is sold, shown to another, or set before any market. It is used only to carry out what the keeper has asked of it.

Who else may see it

No one. There is no second party, no watching service, and no wider circle.

To withdraw it

The keeper may sever this instrument's reach at any time through their own account's permissions.

To reach the keeper

There is one keeper, and one hand behind this notice.

In plain words, for the record

When the keeper links a Google account, Aion reads and changes only the calendar entries and task items the keeper directs it to, and only in order to do what the keeper has asked.

That information is not sold, shared, published, used for advertising, or used to train any model, and no person reads it. It is kept only in the keeper's own storage.

The keeper can end this access at any time from the permissions page of their Google account, which removes it.

Aion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
