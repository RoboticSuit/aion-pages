Notice

This instrument belongs to one keeper and answers to no other.

What it reaches

Only what its single keeper already holds under their own name, entered and read on their behalf alone.

What becomes of it

Nothing is sold, shown to another, or set before any market. It is used only to carry out what the keeper has asked of it.

Who else may see it

No one. There is no second party, no watching service, and no wider circle.

To withdraw it

The keeper may sever this instrument's reach at any time through their own account's permissions.

To reach the keeper

There is one keeper, and one hand behind this notice.
